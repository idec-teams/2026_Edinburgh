X-gluc application

  • Cover colonies ✓

Remaking the 40 mg/ml X-gluc stock solution: 0.025 g (25 mg) into 0.625 ml. Working solution: 20 µl of the 40 mg/ml stock mixed with 80 µl sterile water ✓. Spread over colonies ✓ — 5 µl on each colony ✓.
