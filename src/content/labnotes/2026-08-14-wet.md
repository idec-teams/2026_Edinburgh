OD600 of the 24 h cultures from 13 August:

| Culture | OD600 |
|---|---|
| 1a — glucose + beta-glucosidase | 1.88 |
| 1b — glucose + beta-glucosidase | 1.86 |
| 2a — glucose + CxnA | 1.76 |
| 2b — glucose + CxnA | 1.88 |
| 3a — cellobiose + CxnA | 0.250 |
| 3b — cellobiose + CxnA | 0.210 |
| 4a — cellobiose + beta-glucosidase | 0.530 |
| 4b — cellobiose + beta-glucosidase | 0.530 |

Both glucose conditions reached comparable, high density regardless of strain; both cellobiose conditions ended up substantially lower, with CxnA (3a/3b) even lower than beta-glucosidase (4a/4b).

Serial dilutions (10⁻³, 10⁻⁴, 10⁻⁵) of each culture were plated on LB + 40 µg/mL chloramphenicol for CFU counting the following incubation period.
